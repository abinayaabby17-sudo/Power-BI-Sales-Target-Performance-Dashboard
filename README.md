# Sales & Target Performance Dashboard | Power BI

## Project Overview

This Power BI portfolio project analyzes sales performance against business targets across different regions and products.

The dashboard was designed to help management quickly understand overall sales performance, identify target achievement gaps, compare regional performance, and monitor monthly sales trends.

**Tool:** Microsoft Power BI  
**Data Source:** Excel  
**Project Type:** Practice / Portfolio Project

## Business Objectives

The dashboard was developed to answer the following questions:

- What is the overall sales performance?
- How is actual sales performance compared with the target?
- Which regions are performing better or worse against their targets?
- What is the Q1 sales variance?
- What percentage of the Q1 target has been achieved?
- How are sales trending month by month?
- How can performance be filtered by region and product?

## Dashboard Features

The dashboard includes:

- KPI cards for key sales metrics
- Monthly Sales Trend from January to July 2026
- Q1 Actual vs Target comparison by Region
- Sales by Region analysis
- Regional performance matrix
- Q1 Variance analysis
- Q1 Achievement %
- Region slicer
- Product slicer
- Interactive filtering between dashboard visuals

## Key Insights

- Total sales reached approximately **1.43M**.
- High-value sales contributed approximately **1.13M**.
- Q1 actual sales were **510K** against a target of **1.55M**.
- Overall Q1 target achievement was **32.90%**.
- Q1 sales were approximately **1.04M below target**.
- **West** recorded the highest Q1 actual sales and achieved **48.75%** of its target.
- **South** achieved **47.14%** of its Q1 target.
- **North** showed the largest relative performance gap, achieving only **5.00%** of its Q1 target.
- The monthly sales trend shows the strongest sales performance in **June 2026**.
